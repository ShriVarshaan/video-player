# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The first release of Cinewall, a player for the movies and shows on your own PC.

- Your films on an endless wall of posters you can pan in any direction.
- A page for each show, with its seasons and episodes, and Continue watching.
- Clean, easy-to-read subtitles, downloads from OpenSubtitles, and auto sync that lines subtitles up with the speech.
- Search as you type.
- Volume up to 200%.
- A short setup when you first open it, and updates you choose whether to install.
