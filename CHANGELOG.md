# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- Picture in picture: press P, or the new button left of Audio, and the film keeps playing in a small window on top while you work in other apps.
- Drag the small window anywhere, resize it from its edges and corners, and it opens in the same spot next time.
- Press P again, Esc, or double-click the picture to go back to the full-size player.
- Settings → About: the mpv and FFmpeg source link works again. It now points to a copy kept with Cinewall's own releases.

## 1.1.0

- Press C while watching to change the aspect ratio, going through the same ratios as VLC. Press Shift+C to go straight back to the film's own ratio.
- The ratio in use shows at the top right, and subtitles move with the picture.

## 1.0.0

The first release of Cinewall, a player for the movies and shows on your own PC.

- Your films on an endless wall of posters you can pan in any direction.
- A page for each show, with its seasons and episodes, and Continue watching.
- Clean, easy-to-read subtitles, downloads from OpenSubtitles, and auto sync that lines subtitles up with the speech.
- Search as you type.
- Volume up to 200%.
- A short setup when you first open it, and updates you choose whether to install.
